# Horizon Atelier Company Rule · Gross Margin
STD-102 · v1.2 · Synthetic pricing policy

Gross margin = (pre-tax selling price − direct cost) ÷ pre-tax selling price.
Pre-tax selling price = direct cost ÷ (1 − target gross margin).

## Example
Direct cost NT$60,000, target margin 25%:
- Pre-tax selling price = NT$80,000
- Gross profit = NT$20,000
- Sample 5% tax = NT$4,000
- Tax inclusive sample amount = NT$84,000.

25% margin is NOT the same as adding 25% markup to cost.
Owner must check real cost base, contingency, scope inclusions, VAT treatment and supplier validity. No AI or demo calculator may bypass human approval.
