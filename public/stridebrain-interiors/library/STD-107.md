# Horizon Atelier Company Rule · Customer Response
STD-107 · v1.2 · Synthetic communications policy

Customer replies should identify the correct project, latest approved drawings and referenced quotation revision. A message can propose next steps but must not invent stock, availability, prices or lead times. Commercial terms require owner approval; material substitutions require a controlled change order. Drafts are reviewed before copying or sending. A reviewed draft is not proof of delivery.

For Songshan quartz CHG-101-QZ: cite DOC-1006 (base specification) and DOC-1010 (unverified supplier estimate). Before commercial approval, explain that pricing is being checked. A new completion date is not confirmed.
Local demo response review is not actual customer authorization.
