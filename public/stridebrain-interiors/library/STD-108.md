# Site inspection and safety — Horizon Atelier internal standard

Standard ID: STD-108
Revision: v1.4
Status: Approved for synthetic demo

## Rule
The responsible staff member must refer to an approved record, document the decision and its source, and obtain explicit authorization for any price, scope, schedule, safety or customer commitment.

## Evidence required
- Reference the associated project ID and approved document version.
- Cite the relevant quotation, supplier, work order or contractual record.
- Flag missing or conflicting inputs and avoid inventing precision.
- Preserve audit history for user-visible decisions.

## Exceptions
Escalate conflicts, unusually high costs, delayed materials and changes to agreed work. Never treat a draft or synthetic supplier price as a confirmed third-party offer.

This is a fictional example policy for demonstration, not legal or professional advice.
