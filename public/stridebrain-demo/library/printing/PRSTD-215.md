# Delivery exceptions

Policy: PRSTD-215
Version: v1.1

## Required checks
Verify the job identity, latest approved specification, quotation revision and responsible sign-off. If any source is missing, refer the decision for review; do not generate fabricated stock, pricing, certification or delivery facts.

## Approval principle
A customer inquiry can generate a proposed response, but production work or customer commitments require a reviewed record. Preserve the decision log.

## Example applicability
Print job JOB-201: recycled paper substitution requires material availability, recalculated price and written customer approval before updating the print ticket.

All policies are fictional demo examples.
