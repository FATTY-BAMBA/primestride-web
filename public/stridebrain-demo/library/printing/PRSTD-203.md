# Clearprint Studio Company Rule · Quotation Margins
PRSTD-203 · v1.2 · Fictional print-shop policy

Pre-tax selling price = attributable direct cost / (1 - target gross margin).
Recycled stock change example: NT$2,400 direct cost / 0.70 = NT$3,428.57, rounded to NT$3,429. Example 5% tax rounds to NT$171; total NT$3,600.
Margin and markup are different; amounts exclude any omitted cost categories and require sign-off.
Use current, confirmed stock and press costs in real customer quotations.
