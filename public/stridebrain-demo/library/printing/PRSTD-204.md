# Clearprint Studio Company Rule · Artwork Acceptance
PRSTD-204 · v1.1 · Fictional operating policy

Before production release, confirm: latest PDF/AI proof version, resolution at final trim, 3 mm bleed, CMYK profile, overprint, type outlines/embedded fonts, quantity, finishing instruction, approved paper stock and customer proof acceptance.
A material substitution creates a new proof/production revision. Do not use an unapproved revision as an operating instruction. Retain proof source and reviewer identity.
