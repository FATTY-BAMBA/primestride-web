# 2026 新制消息查核 / Avoid incorrect statements about 2026 permit changes

## Evidence-based explanation
The national authority publicly corrected circulating claims that a newly imposed 2026 rule universally changed the start-work permit procedure; it stated that the discussed contract provisions were then a draft and existing application rules had not changed.
