## Evidence-based explanation
Article 33 sets conditional pathways based on work characteristics, area, floor, fire compartments and unaffected safety systems. It is not a general DIY exemption.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Collect floor, gross floor area and current use
- Record whether life-safety elements change
- Identify professional signatories
- Route regulatory pathway choice for local expert review

## Questions StrideBrain should ask
- Does the project satisfy every stated eligibility condition?
- What does the local building authority require?

## Scope and uncertainty
Software should flag possible relevance only; not make a binding permit determination.
