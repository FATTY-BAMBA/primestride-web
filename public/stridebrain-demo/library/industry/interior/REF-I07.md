# 設計變更與核定圖說 / Design revisions and approved construction instructions

Reference ID: REF-I07
Category: REGULATION_PLUS_WORKFLOW
Workflow phase: Change order
Source status: CURATED PUBLIC INDUSTRY REFERENCE (not company-specific)
Last verified: 2026-10-18

## Evidence-based explanation
For regulated works, Article 30 addresses work according to approved drawings and how design changes are handled. The recommended product workflow is to hold draft drawings until reviewed, track downstream impact, and prevent unapproved work instructions.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Show original and revised drawing versions
- Flag changed safety/material details
- Estimate cost and schedule effects
- Hold construction-release until required decisions are recorded

## Questions StrideBrain should ask
- Is this an approved drawing or a draft pricing option?
- Must the revised drawing be resubmitted for review?

## Scope and uncertainty
Resubmission requirements are conditional; professional decision needed.
