# 室內裝修圖說與審查資料 / Drawing package and submission requirements

Category: REGULATION
Workflow phase: Design & review

## Evidence-based explanation
Articles 23–26 describe required applications, evidence of property rights, existing authorized plans, and location, plan, elevation, section and detail drawings for applicable review procedures.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Track drawing version, author and approving professional
- Identify missing rights/plan documents
- Preserve approved construction issue
- Track fire and material check requirements

## Questions StrideBrain should ask
- Which drawing is legally current for work?
- What evidence is missing from the application package?

## Scope and uncertainty
The demo is a document readiness assistant, not an approval authority.
