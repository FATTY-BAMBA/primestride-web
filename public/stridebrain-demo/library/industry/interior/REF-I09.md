## Evidence-based explanation
Articles 32–34 cover completion inspection and documents for regulated renovation; the official check form references approved drawings, materials, fire/escape facilities and other checks.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Collect completion photos and authorized drawing issue
- Reconcile work against approved plans
- Identify required inspection and certificate
- Track defects, customer acceptance and warranties as company workflow

## Questions StrideBrain should ask

- Who is authorized to sign the inspection documents?
