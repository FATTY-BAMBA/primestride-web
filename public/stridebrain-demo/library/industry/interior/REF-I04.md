# 消防設備與避難安全檢查 / Fire protection and escape-route safeguards

Reference ID: REF-I04
Category: REGULATION
Workflow phase: Design & review
Source status: CURATED PUBLIC INDUSTRY REFERENCE (not company-specific)
Last verified: 2026-10-18

## Evidence-based explanation
Relevant renovation review checks include maintaining fire/escape facilities, fire compartments, and building structural elements. Fire-equipment changes may involve separate fire authority review.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Ask whether exits/escape paths change
- Flag partitions and fire doors
- Check fire-equipment scope with qualified designer
- Record authority sign-off if applicable

## Questions StrideBrain should ask
- Will a proposed layout alter escape or fire-safety systems?
- Who must validate a safety-related change?

## Scope and uncertainty
Never let the Copilot approve fire design or technical compliance.
