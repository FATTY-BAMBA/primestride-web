# 裝修契約範本與工程範圍 / Design and construction contract structure

Reference ID: REF-I05
Category: OFFICIAL_TEMPLATE
Workflow phase: Commercial proposal
Source status: CURATED PUBLIC INDUSTRY REFERENCE (not company-specific)
Last verified: 2026-10-18

## Evidence-based explanation
The Ministry of the Interior publishes separate sample contracts for design, renovation construction and combined services. Scope, pricing, payment terms and responsibilities need to be aligned to the selected agreement.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Choose design-only, build-only or combined engagement
- Identify work scope and exclusions
- Attach pricing revision and drawing issue
- Document change-order procedure and acceptance parties

## Questions StrideBrain should ask
- Is this a design retainer or a construction commitment?
- Which contract version governs the quotation?

## Scope and uncertainty
A public model form is not a signed customer contract; legal review required for a real engagement.
