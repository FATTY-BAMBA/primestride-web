## Evidence-based explanation
The official contract resources and consumer-protection guidance motivate traceable scope, price and revision review. StrideBrain can show original scope, added work, actual verified costs, proposed price and approval state as separate objects.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Never overwrite the originally approved contract price
- Link change to scope/drawing revision
- Keep contractor/subcontractor cost evidence
- Distinguish payable milestones from paid cash

## Questions StrideBrain should ask
- What has the client approved in writing?
- Does a supplier quote expire before the planned work?

Workflow recommendation derived from public contract context, not a legally mandated universal change form.
