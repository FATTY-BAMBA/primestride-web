## Evidence-based explanation
The business-tax law provides the framework for different tax rates and calculation. The demo's flat 5% is an explicit illustrative assumption, not verification of the client company's actual tax obligations.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Keep net price, tax assumption and gross price separate
- Label budget, estimate, accepted contract and actual collections distinctly
- Owner/accountant verifies tax treatment
- Only approved quotes go to customer

## Questions StrideBrain should ask

- Is this a proposed price or accepted contract?
