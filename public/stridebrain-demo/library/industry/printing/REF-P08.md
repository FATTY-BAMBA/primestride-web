# 印後加工與裝訂 / Finishing and binding as first-class production stages

## Evidence-based explanation
The Taipei printing association describes an ecosystem spanning prepress, press and postpress. Published book guidance demonstrates finishing and binding variation. In StrideBrain, finishing should be an explicit task and cost stage.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Define trim, fold, lamination and binding requirements
- Connect finish choices to stock and schedule
- Inspect defects and tolerance concerns
- Allocate finishing vendor owner and due dates

## Questions StrideBrain should ask
- What post-press process is required?
- Is an external finishing vendor involved?

## Scope and uncertainty
This is a workflow model, not a claim every printer owns every machine.
