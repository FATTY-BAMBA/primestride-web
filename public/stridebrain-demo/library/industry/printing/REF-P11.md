# 紙材替代、供應與價格波動 / Paper substitution and supplier change controls

Reference ID: REF-P11
Category: WORKFLOW_RECOMMENDATION
Workflow phase: Procurement
Source status: CURATED PUBLIC INDUSTRY REFERENCE (not company-specific)
Last verified: 2026-10-18

## Evidence-based explanation
Print choices affect the prepress output, stock, press setup and finishing. The product should hold a paper substitution until the selected supplier and customer approve material performance, availability, price, and proof implications.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Keep original and proposed stock in change request
- Compute direct cost delta separately
- Ask for sample proof if color/absorption changes
- Create new production ticket only after required approvals

## Questions StrideBrain should ask
- Does the new paper need revised color settings or delivery planning?
- Are current supplier costs verified?

## Scope and uncertainty
Sample workflow design, not a claim of universal stock pricing.
