## Evidence-based explanation
A Taiwan printer's book instructions call for separate cover and interior files, PDF settings, binding-dependent cover construction and page arrangement, with distinct examples for saddle stitch and perfect binding.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Capture binding type, spine and page count
- Keep cover and interior file versions separate
- Check binding-specific imposition and bleed
- Record sample proof review before printing

## Questions StrideBrain should ask
- How many pages and which binding type?
- Are files split according to the selected print vendor's requirements?

## Scope and uncertainty
Do not carry a vendor's binding page limits across other presses without verification.
