## Evidence-based explanation
Prepress education in Taiwan explicitly covers file preflight, imposition calculations, job tickets and cost estimation; the demo can expose these concepts as cost lines rather than infer them invisibly.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Identify setup/makeready costs
- Separate material, press, finishing and delivery lines
- Validate print quantity and layout/imposition
- Record scrap and overrun assumptions

## Questions StrideBrain should ask
- What press setup is required?
- How many sellable pieces per sheet and what waste allowance applies?

## Scope and uncertainty
Prices and production speeds must come from the individual company's records.
