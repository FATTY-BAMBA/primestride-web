# 預估與實際印製成本差異 / Historical estimates versus actual manufacturing cost

Reference ID: REF-P12
Category: WORKFLOW_RECOMMENDATION
Workflow phase: Management analytics
Source status: CURATED PUBLIC INDUSTRY REFERENCE (not company-specific)
Last verified: 2026-10-18

## Evidence-based explanation
Costing should separate estimated materials, setup, printing, finishing, packing and dispatch from actual consumption and accepted outputs so future comparison is defensible.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Record estimated and actual cost codes
- Tie reprints/waste to job ID
- Do not treat quoted selling price as cost
- Review deviations before using history to quote new jobs

## Questions StrideBrain should ask
- Was the job accepted and cost-complete?
- Did reprint losses affect actual margins?

## Scope and uncertainty
The demo's generated cost histories are fictional and cannot prove real market price.
