## Evidence-based explanation
A historic government printing contract illustrates repeated proof review and a full sample approved before final printing. It is a concrete contract example, not a universal law for all jobs.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Track proof version and correction round
- Record acceptance of color/trim/sample
- Block press-release without required client sign-off
- Preserve issued-to-production file checksum or revision

## Questions StrideBrain should ask
- Has the customer approved the correct proof revision?
- Does the signed contract require a physical proof?
