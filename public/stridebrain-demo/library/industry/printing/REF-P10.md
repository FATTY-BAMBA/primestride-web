# 油墨、溶劑與空污適用檢核 / Printing environmental compliance screening

Reference ID: REF-P10
Category: REGULATORY_SCREENING
Workflow phase: Operations compliance
Source status: CURATED PUBLIC INDUSTRY REFERENCE (not company-specific)
Last verified: 2026-10-18

## Evidence-based explanation
Printing processes using solvent-based inks, cleansers or coatings may trigger environmental emission control, depending on the regulated process and amounts. Historical environmental correspondence illustrates why ink/solvent usage must be reviewed.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo
- Identify printing process type and relevant materials
- Record annual consumption and SDS
- Request environmental specialist applicability determination
- Track permits and emission-control obligations where required

## Questions StrideBrain should ask
- Does the specific site/process trigger a permit threshold?
- What current ministry/local authority rules apply?

## Scope and uncertainty
Never declare all printers need VOC permits; do not reuse an old letter as current compliance advice.
