## Evidence-based explanation
Printing quotations and customer contact files need applicable tax treatment and purpose-limited handling of personal data; a sample 5% assumption requires validation for actual trade.

## Suggested controls for the StrideBrain demo

- Protect buyer contacts, shipping information and artwork
- Define who sees confidential client orders
- Approve outbound communications
