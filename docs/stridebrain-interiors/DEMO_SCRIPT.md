# Suggested Sales Walkthrough (6–8 minutes)

1. **Meet the company (45s)**: Horizon Atelier has twelve projects, hundreds of connected records and a portfolio that demands owner attention.
2. **Ask one useful question (60s)**: 'What needs my attention today?' Show derived approval and blocked-task counts, not model-generated estimates.
3. **Open a real source (60s)**: Inspect DOC-1006 (Materials Schedule) and DOC-1010 (Supplier Quotation), show revisions, owners and the fact that these are synthetic.
4. **Show the cost of a change (90s)**: PRJ-101 quartz upgrade, default direct cost NT$60,000 and target gross margin 25%. Quote is NT$80,000 before sample 5% tax. Try changing the input.
5. **Prove governance (90s)**: Attempt to commit early: blocked. Simulate supplier confirmation, owner approval and customer approval, then commit.
6. **Show downstream state (60s)**: WO-VAR-101 appears in Work Orders; analytics updates. Refresh and show browser persistence.
7. **Explain the production roadmap (60s)**: Distinguish the working prototype from planned real AI, file upload, databases, permissions and approved messaging.

Suggested customer question: 'Which parts of your day still depend on someone remembering the latest version, cost or approval?'

Never claim the prototype has live supplier data, true AI inference, customer messaging, or production credentials.
