# Research-backed Taiwan SME Demonstrations · 2026-10-18
## Why public industry evidence is distinct from fictional company data

The customer-facing promise is a shared knowledge foundation across knowledge answers, customer communications, quoting, work tracking, and analytics. Before real client onboarding, StrideBrain can demonstrate plausible daily operations by combining (1) source-linked public evidence and (2) internally consistent fictitious company records. A public standard or vendor tutorial is never evidence of a fictional company's permit, approved pricing, procurement, past costs, or actual transactions.

## Interior design and renovation: how a responsible workflow is modeled

1. Lead intake: building use/location, owner consent, conceptual needs, budget and target timing.
2. Qualified screening: fixed walls/ceilings/partitions and fire-safety changes trigger professional applicability review under Taiwan renovation law. Avoid a blanket statement that every decorative change requires the same filing.
3. Existing conditions and design documents: retrieve current approved plans, site dimensions, material requirements and responsible signatories; flag superseded drawings.
4. Proposal and contract: choose design-only/build-only/combined service scope; separate proposed price, VAT assumption, exclusions and signed agreement. The Ministry of Interior publishes model contracts; the template is not itself an executed agreement.
5. Procurement and trades: validate materials, supplier quote validity, installation risk and labor, creating work instructions only from accepted scope.
6. Site and change coordination: track approved versus draft drawing versions, change-order price and schedule impacts, client review and trade handoff.
7. Completion and handover: match work to approved drawings and relevant regulated inspection requirements, with defects and warranty details documented by authorized parties.
8. Improvement: compare reviewed estimated versus actual costs to improve future quotation. This requires genuine verified client data, not fictional prices.

### Flagship sample story: Songshan kitchen quartz
Question: Can a customer switch from solid-surface to quartz? What is the cost and will handover move?
Relevant sources in the demo: approved materials schedule, draft drawing revision, unverified synthetic supplier quote, modelled change-order, official regulatory considerations.
Response: show scope differences, measured dimensions and supplier uncertainty, a clearly fictional cost estimate and a quote margin calculation. Require a professional to determine legal/permit effects, owner commercial approval and written customer acceptance. Only then create a work order; never issue a legally valid permit or promise revised dates automatically.

## Printing/packaging: how a practical job workflow is modeled

1. Inquiry: product category, quantity, finished size, paper, colors, one/two-sided, finishing and due date.
2. Prepress intake: verify format, trim dimensions, bleed, font embedding, placed image resolution, orientation and color mode according to the agreed vendor/job requirements.
3. Proofing: preserve proof versions and customer approval; color changes and material substitutions require updated proof review where appropriate.
4. Quotation: separate paper cost, prepress/imposition, press setup, inks, finishing, packing and delivery. Use actual production yield and waste assumptions for real price commitments.
5. Material/proof/schedule gate: release a job ticket only when stock, proof approval, manufacturing capacity and price terms are confirmed.
6. Press and finishing work: explicit work centers, outsource instructions, quality samples and defect/reprint handling.
7. Delivery and acceptance: dispatch the approved quantity and inspect according to customer contract.
8. Closeout learning: reconcile actual material usage, setup, reprints, output and direct costs to make historic quotations useful.

- Taipei Printing Commercial Association (industry covers prepress, press and postpress): https://www.tcpa88.org.tw/gong-hui-jie-shao/gong-hui-jian-jie
- Taiwan printer poster/postcard PDF guidelines (vendor example, not universal): https://www.interprint.com.tw/docInfo.aspx?docid=63&key=c3

### Flagship sample story: 500 A3 posters on recycled paper
Question: Is a supplied print PDF press-ready, and can 150gsm coated stock be replaced with recycled 170gsm paper without delay?
Public vendor example: A3 finished size 297 × 420mm; with 3mm four-side bleed, a specific printer shows 303 × 426mm artwork size; fonts should be embedded/outlined and 300dpi or better is recommended. RGB may still print according to this vendor but introduces conversion risk, so it should not be categorically rejected. Another vendor or product may have different specifications.
Product simulation: note missing preflight information, store the correct proof revision, ask for actual paper availability and a current supplier quote, calculate incremental synthetic costs and mark the production schedule unconfirmed. Customer must approve changed price and proof before sending to production. Never claim that an uploaded file has been digitally checked if the demo simply records manual answers.

## Shared constraints across both packs

- Tax in demo uses a labeled example 5% assumption. Real applicability and invoicing need professional validation: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=G0340080&sid=1QUgcF
- Customer addresses, messages and pricing cannot become a globally shared training corpus. Taiwan privacy requirements and tenant isolation matter: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=I0050021
- Public industry sources are summarized/paraphrased; licensing, terms and publication changes must be respected.
- A modelled procedure is a product-design recommendation unless an original authority explicitly requires it.
- Legal, safety, tax and supplier applicability decisions require qualified human verification.
- Every operational metric must indicate whether it is a draft forecast, budget, invoice, actual cost, or collected cash.

## Evidence source metadata requirements
Reference ID; official/publisher title; URL; publisher; source class (law/official template/industry association/vendor example/historical contract); scoped locator/article; as-of date; applicability warning; links to workflow stage and demo record. All reference summaries use original wording and preserve a hyperlink to the source.

## Before expanding to more sectors
Run the same process for manufacturing, trading and professional services: research authoritative Taiwan sources, interview representative practitioners where possible, model work/cost/approval objects, implement minimum workflows with fictional but consistent sample records, then prove source correctness and tenant isolation. Do not market an unused industry tab as a complete template.
